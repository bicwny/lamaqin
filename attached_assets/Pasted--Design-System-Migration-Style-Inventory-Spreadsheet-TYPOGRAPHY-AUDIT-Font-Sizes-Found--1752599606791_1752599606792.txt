## MIGRATION RISK ASSESSMENT:

### Low Risk (Isolated Components):
- practice-detail/[practiceId].tsx ✓ (Already audited)
- meditation-detail/[recordId].tsx
- Individual modal components

### Medium Risk (Shared but Contained):
- PageTemplate.tsx (affects all screens but contained)
- ModalTemplate.tsx (affects all modals but contained)
- Card components (reusable but predictable)

### High Risk (Core Infrastructure):
- ThemedText.tsx (affects entire app typography)
- ThemedView.tsx (affects entire app colors)
- _layout.tsx files (affects navigation and theme)

### Critical Risk (Breaking Changes):
- Colors.ts removal (affects every file that imports it)
- DesignSystem.ts major changes (affects all migrated files)
