三殊胜 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 4 月 28 日

三殊胜（以下简称"本应用"）是一款为佛教修行者提供日常修行记录、课程学习与统计的工具。 我们尊重并保护用户的个人隐私。本政策说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储与保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 您主动提供的信息

  • 账户信息：注册时提供的电子邮箱与密码。密码以加密形式存储于身份验证服务，本应用无法看到明文密码。
  • 个人资料：法名（可选）、所属班级或课程等修学相关资料。
  • 修行记录：您所记录的功课名称、计数、时长、日期、备注等。

1.2 自动产生的信息

  • 本地通知：用于您本人设置的修行提醒；通知内容仅在您的设备上生成，不会上传服务器。
  • 应用基本运行数据：仅为支持登录会话与数据同步所必需的最小信息。

1.3 我们不收集的信息

本应用不收集您的位置信息、通讯录、相机/相册、麦克风、健康数据、广告标识符（IDFA），也不接入任何第三方广告或分析 SDK。

2. 信息的使用

我们仅将上述信息用于以下目的：

  • 为您提供账户登录、修行记录同步与课程进度展示；
  • 在您本人设备上发送修行提醒通知；
  • 保障账户与数据安全（如防止未经授权访问）。

我们不会将您的个人信息用于广告投放，也不会出售或出租给任何第三方。

3. 信息的存储与第三方服务

本应用使用 Supabase 作为身份验证与数据库服务提供商。您的账户与修行记录数据存储于 Supabase 托管的数据库中， 传输过程使用 HTTPS/TLS 加密。请同时参阅 Supabase 的隐私政策。

除 Supabase 之外，本应用未集成任何其他后端、广告或分析第三方服务。

4. 您的权利

  • 查看与修改：您可在应用内随时查看与修改个人资料和修行记录。
  • 删除账户：如希望删除账户与全部相关数据，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系，我们将在合理期限内处理。
  • 撤回授权：您可在系统设置中关闭通知权限。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会主动收集其个人信息。如发现误收集情况，我们将尽快删除。

6. 政策变更

本政策可能随产品功能调整而更新，更新后的版本将以此页面发布日期为准。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策或您的数据有任何疑问，请联系：fob.design@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: April 28, 2026

三殊胜 ("Sanshusheng", the "App") is a personal tool that helps Buddhist practitioners track daily practices, courses, and progress. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account: email address and password used at sign-up. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our auth provider; we never see your plain-text password.
  • Profile: dharma name (optional) and class/course enrollment information you choose to enter.
  • Practice records: the practice name, counts, durations, dates, and notes that you log.

1.2 Information generated automatically

  • Local notifications: reminders you schedule yourself. Notification content is generated on your device and is not sent to any server.
  • Minimal session data required to keep you signed in and to sync your records.

1.3 What we do NOT collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, camera/photo library, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier (IDFA). The App does not integrate any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide sign-in, sync your practice records, and show course progress.
  • To deliver local reminder notifications you schedule.
  • To protect your account and data (e.g. preventing unauthorized access).

We do not use your personal information for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

3. Storage and Third Parties

The App uses Supabase for authentication and database storage. Your account and practice records are stored in Supabase-hosted infrastructure and transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Please review Supabase's privacy policy as well.

No other third-party backend, advertising, or analytics service is integrated.

4. Your Rights

  • Access & edit: view and edit your profile and practice records inside the App at any time.
  • Account deletion: to delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address below; we will process the request within a reasonable time.
  • Notifications: you can revoke notification permission in your device settings at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact fob.design@gmail.com.
